Mechanical Shaft Coupling (Water Turbine → AC Alternator)

We cannot bring a rushing river indoors. Instead, a 12V RS-775 High-Torque DC Motor acts as our "Water Turbine". We lock its 5mm shaft directly to a NEMA 17 Stepper Motor using a 5mm-to-5mm Brass Coupler. When the DC motor spins at 1500 RPM, the stepper motor is forced to spin with it.

PART #3 • MECHANICAL ~NPR 150 (New Road / Himalayan Solution)

5mm-to-5mm Brass Shaft Coupler

🏔️ Real Nepal Micro-Hydro Role:

Main steel drive shaft connecting the Pelton/Crossflow water turbine runner to the synchronous AC alternator rotor.

🔬 Our Exhibition Bench Role:

Locks the 5mm shaft of our RS-775 DC motor directly to the 5mm shaft of our NEMA 17 stepper motor so they spin at the exact same RPM.

⚡ Exact Wiring & Assembly Spec:

Slide halfway onto 775 DC Motor 5mm shaft & halfway onto NEMA 17 5mm shaft. Tighten the 4 M3 hex grub screws firmly. Ensure straight coaxial alignment to prevent vibration.
